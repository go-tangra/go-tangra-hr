You need `hr:request` to request leave (every member of the tenant has it
by default, through the *HR employee* role). HR must have set up at least one absence
type, and, for types that deduct days, an allowance for you this year.

## 1. Check your balance

Open **My leave**. The **Balance** card shows, for the chosen year and each
absence type or pool you have an allowance for: total days, days carried
over from last year, used, pending (requested but not yet approved) and
remaining.

## 2. Request leave

1. Choose **New request**.
2. Choose the **Absence type**.
3. Enter the **First day** and **Last day**. For half days, tick **Half first
   day** or **Half last day** (for a single day: **Half day**).
4. Optionally add a **Reason** and **Notes** (notes are visible to your
   approvers and HR).

Before you submit, the form shows:

- how many days the request counts (Monday to Friday, without public
  holidays);
- how many days you have left after it, per year;
- who will approve it, or "No approval needed: approved when you submit";
- a warning if it overlaps another of your requests.

Choose **Submit request**. The request is **Pending** and your approver gets
an email. For a type that needs no approval it is **Approved** at once and
the days are taken from your allowance.

## 3. Follow the request

Open the request from **My leave** to see its status, approvers and, once
decided, who decided and their notes. You get an email when it is approved,
rejected or revoked. Approved and pending leave also shows on the **Leave
calendar**.

## Change your mind

| Status | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Pending | **Edit** reason and notes; **Cancel request** |
| Awaiting signing | **Cancel request** |
| Approved, not yet started | **Cancel request**: the days go back to your allowance |
| Approved and started | ask your approver or HR to revoke it |
| Rejected or cancelled | **Delete** it from your list |

To change the dates, cancel the request and file a new one.

## Common refusals

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| overlap | you already have a pending or approved request on one of these days |
| insufficient_allowance | not enough days left in that year |
| no_allowance | you have no allowance for this type (or its pool) in that year; ask HR |
| zero_days | the dates contain no working day |
